Blastoid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Blastoid ("the App") is a block-puzzle game published by Yamango Labs. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used. Blastoid is designed to work fully offline and does not require an account, login, or any personal registration.

Information We Collect

Game data (stored only on your device). Your scores, saved games, level progress, daily challenge streaks, and settings (language, sound, haptics, theme) are stored locally on your device using an on-device database. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

Advertising data. The App shows banner and rewarded-video ads served by Google AdMob. The App requests non-personalized ads only. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may process technical identifiers (such as an advertising ID or IP-derived general location) on your device. We do not receive or store this data ourselves — it is processed directly by Google under its own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. You can review Google's ad technology providers here: AdMob ad partners.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration, name, email address, or phone number
  • No precise GPS location
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • No sale of personal data

Children's Privacy

Blastoid is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because gameplay data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time from within the App's Settings screen ("Reset progress") or by uninstalling the App.

Your Choices

You can limit ad personalization at the device level through your Android or iOS system settings (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization" / "Delete advertising ID").

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
